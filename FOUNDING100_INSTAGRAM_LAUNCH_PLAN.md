## A. Launch principles (empty account → credible fast)
1. **Don't launch onto an empty grid.** Seed 3 foundation posts first so anyone who clicks through from a DM/ad sees a real brand, not a ghost town.
2. **Profile does the selling 24/7** — bio + link + highlights must state the offer and the scarcity.
3. **Every post earns the next action** — DM "MENU", visit link, or tag a restaurant. No post without a CTA.
4. **Scarcity is the engine** — a visible "X / 100 claimed" runs through the whole sprint. Keep the number real.
5. **Show the product moving** — video/transformation out-performs static. Lead with motion where possible.
6. **2 feed posts/day + 2–3 story beats/day** through the 10-day sprint.

## C. Campaign content pillars (Instagram)
1. **Transformation** — boring PDF → cinema menu (before/after; highest scroll-stopping value).
2. **Show-don't-tell** — dish videos, the menu moving on a phone.
3. **Owner outcomes/proof** — order more, faster service, tourists order confidently; founder spotlights.
4. **Education/authority** — menu psychology, QR/tourist trends, why video sells (saves + shares).
5. **Founding 100 / FOMO** — scarcity counter, "first 10 founders," countdown, "spots left."

## D. Content matrix (format × pillar × purpose)
| Format | Best pillars | Purpose | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reel (15–25s) | Transformation, Show, FOMO | Reach + wow (top of funnel) | ~1/day |
| Carousel (5–7 slides) | Education, Proof, Transformation | Saves/shares + depth | ~1/day |
| Static post | FOMO, Offer, Quote/stat | Announce + scarcity | as needed |
| Stories (3–5 frames) | FOMO, BTS, Polls, Spotlight | Daily heartbeat + DMs | 2–3 beats/day |

## E. Scheduling (how to actually run it)
- **Tool:** Meta **Business Suite Planner** (free, native — schedules feed posts, Reels, and Stories) is the default. For a nicer visual grid + analytics, **Metricool** or **Later** (free tiers cover this).
- **Workflow:** we prep every post (caption + hashtags + the generated image/reel) in a simple calendar/sheet → bulk-load into Business Suite at the start of each week → Stories posted daily (some story interactions are best done live for polls/countdown stickers).
- **Best Dublin posting windows:** ~12:00–13:00 (lunch) and ~18:00–20:00 (dinner decisions). Put the AM value post at lunch, the PM offer/FOMO post in the evening.
