## 1. What an "affiliate bank" actually is

It is a **done-for-you toolkit** that an affiliate downloads on Day 1, drops their own affiliate link into, and starts using — without writing a single piece of original content. The shorter the gap between "they joined" and "they made their first sale," the more affiliates promote you.

Three principles every great affiliate bank follows:

1. **Plug-and-play.** Every asset must work with one find-and-replace edit. Affiliates won't write copy — they'll copy yours.
2. **Multi-channel coverage.** Affiliates differ: some run email lists, some do Instagram, some run paid ads, some do cold DMs, some host webinars. Cover all of them.
3. **Multiple tones per asset.** Affiliates promoting to fine dining are different from affiliates promoting to food trucks. Give 3–5 versions of every key asset.

**Audience variants (P0):**

## 5. Commission program — recommended structure

The commission structure matters as much as the assets. Affiliates will read this first.

### Option A — Aggressive / land-grab (recommended for first 12 months)

- **40% recurring** on the affiliate's referred restaurants, **for the lifetime** of the account
- **Plus a €50 one-time bonus** at the moment of paid signup (paid after restaurant's first €39 month)
- **Bonus tiers:**
  - 10 active paying restaurants → +€500 cash bonus
  - 25 active → +€1,500 cash + featured spotlight
  - 50 active → +€5,000 cash + named partner status
  - 100 active → €10,000 + trip to Dublin + lifetime 50% bump (40% → 50%)

**Why aggressive:** in the first 12 months you want affiliate momentum, not margin protection. GoHighLevel built a $1B business doing exactly this.

### Option B — Standard SaaS

- 30% recurring for 24 months
- No upfront bonus
- Smaller milestone bonuses

### Option C — Per-restaurant flat fee

- €80 per paying restaurant signup (first month invoice paid)
- Easier to budget, less recurring upside for affiliates

**Recommendation:** Start with **Option A**, with the explicit framing "this is the Founding Affiliate program — the first 50 affiliates lock in 40% lifetime even when we lower it later."

## 6. Affiliate onboarding flow

The day someone signs up as an affiliate, they should hit this sequence:

1. **Day 0 — Application approved email** — welcome, here's your tracking link, here's the bank
2. **Day 0 — Welcome video** plays automatically on the dashboard
3. **Day 1 — Email:** "Your first 3 actions today" → 3 specific things (pick 3 restaurants, send DM #1, post Reel #1)
4. **Day 3 — Email:** "Why 80% of affiliates fail by Day 7 (and how to be in the 20%)"
5. **Day 7 — Email:** "Made your first sale yet? If not, do this." + free 15-min call with you
6. **Day 14 — Email:** Showcase a top-performing affiliate's playbook
7. **Day 30 — Email:** "Where you are vs. your bonus tier" + leaderboard nudge

Plus:
- **WhatsApp / Telegram group** for affiliates → real-time wins, questions, support
- **Monthly group call** with Diego — Q&A, what's working, contest winners
- **Affiliate-only feature drops** — "you get to promote this 2 weeks before the public sees it"

## 9. The 4 metrics to track from day one

1. **Affiliate sign-ups per week** — top of funnel
2. **% of affiliates who make their first sale within 14 days** — activation (industry avg ~15%, target 30%)
3. **Average paying restaurants referred per active affiliate** — productivity
4. **Affiliate-driven revenue as % of total revenue** — strategic indicator (target 25–40% within 12 months)

---

## 10. Three rules to enforce — no exceptions

1. **No purchased email lists.** Single-handedly kills the program's deliverability and brand.
2. **No misleading claims.** "Triple your revenue" → instant ban. "Most restaurants see X improvement based on Y" → fine if substantiated.
3. **No brand bidding on Google Ads.** Affiliates buying "Foodlens" as a keyword cannibalizes your direct traffic — common abuse, easy to police.

## The bottom line

The affiliate bank is a **scaling engine**. Founding 100 gets you proof. The affiliate bank turns that proof into 10x distribution at near-zero variable cost.

Don't aim for perfection on Day 1. **Ship the Week 1 MVP** (12 assets above), get 20 affiliates promoting, watch what they actually use and ask for, then build the rest based on demand. The platforms making millions iterated this way too — they just had a head start.

**Start with the welcome video + 5 cold emails + the 7-day checklist. Everything else compounds from there.**
